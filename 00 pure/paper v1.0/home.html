Paper Layout

This is an offshoot of the 'Pure Layout' which is an achromatic layout theme that Lav put together for completely free use to take, change, mangle, and throw across the internet as one might so damn well please to do.

To get a paper-like effect, I use FireAlpaca. Prepare your base image, duplicate layer, toggle alpha lock, set your primary and secondary colors to high contrast colors like black and white, apply the sand effect under filters, apply overlay in layer blending mode, and adjust until you are satisfied with the outcome.

The background image here uses a black sand filter while everything else uses a white sand filter.
